**Dashboard > Build > Members** · Chains: all

Invite teammates to collaborate on this app. The owner is always listed first.

---

## Roles

| Role | Permissions |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Everything, including managing members. One per app. |
| **Admin** | Same as the owner, including what editors cannot do: manage members (other admins included), request mainnet access, withdraw from the distribution vault, accept the app-custody terms for ramps, and archive or restore the app. |
| **Editor** | Read and write: can change the app's settings. |
| **Viewer** | Read only: sees everything, changes nothing. |

---

## Managing members

Everyone can view the member list. The **owner or an admin** can:

- **Invite member**: enter the teammate's email and pick a role. The teammate must already have a Pollar dashboard account.
- Change a member's role from the role selector on their row.
- Remove a member. They lose access immediately. The owner row cannot be removed.

While the app is archived the list is read-only until the app is restored.

Account-level settings (your profile, passkeys, Personal Access Tokens and your own billing) live in the account menu, outside the per-app sidebar.
