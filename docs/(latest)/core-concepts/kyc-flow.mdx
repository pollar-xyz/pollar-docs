Pollar runs identity verification as ordered steps: every step is required, and Pollar talks to the providers so your app does not. This page plays a verification end to end. Pick who asks for the steps, which steps they are, the user's document and the outcome: the diagram rebuilds the flow for that combination.

Your app's own KYC takes Didit and form steps, configured under [Identity checks > Verification steps](https://docs.pollar.xyz/docs/operator-guide/identity-checks/steps). A ramp route or a card provider can also require a SEGIP check of a Bolivian ID card, shown per method under [Cash in and out > Countries and methods](https://docs.pollar.xyz/docs/operator-guide/ramps/methods#identity-check). Prices come from your app's price list, see [Credits](https://docs.pollar.xyz/docs/core-concepts/credits).
